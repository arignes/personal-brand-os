## Lanes — what I engage with

Simple three-level check before replying to or posting about anything:

- ✅ **My lane** (engage fully): AI for marketing, GTM, Web3 growth, build-in-public, marketing tools & costs
- ⚠️ **Adjacent** (engage carefully — comment on the pattern, never pick a side): AI industry news, crypto market takes, hiring/layoffs in tech
- 🚫 **Never** (no reply is worth it, even a smart one): politics, drama between people, scandals, token shilling, doom/hype extremes

Two extra rules:
1. If the author is known for political/drama content — skip even if the post itself is in my lane. The same point will come from a cleaner account soon.
2. If my natural reply would sound like lecturing people — skip.
