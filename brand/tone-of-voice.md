## North star

**Fei-Fei Li, "The Worlds I See"**: the scholar braided with the memoirist. Analytical backbone, but emotion and image are allowed in. That blend is the target register for everything brand-related.

## The core formula

**Simple surface, deep engine.** Simple words everyone understands, arranged so the intelligence shows through; vocabulary depth visible but never showy. Grammatically flawless. Good flow.

Practical rule for drafts: **"plain sentence, one expensive word."** (From the Q11 test: C's honesty and rhythm + one or two of B's precise technical terms. Never resume-speak like "architected and deployed functional internal tooling".)

Important inversion: Arina **speaks plain but writes rich** — the page is where the literary register comes out. Don't sand drafts down to her speaking voice.

## Two registers + the blend

| Register | When | Sounds like |
|---|---|---|
| Analytic | MBA, professional analysis | Diplomat structure: thesis → arguments both ways → resolution. Mostly neutral, but always plants a flag ("I do believe…") |
| Emotional/personal | Stories, personal-lane posts | Detailed, example-rich, feelings named directly ("I think, I feel, I keep coming back to") |
| **Brand blend** | LinkedIn/X posts | Analytic skeleton + personal stake. First person present. Plain words, deep engine. |

## Signature moves

1. **"X can do a lot, but it can't do Y."** Concede the capability, then name the limit. Her natural argument shape — use it.
2. **Plant a flag in neutral territory.** Even analytical posts carry one explicit "I believe" claim — never pure summary.
3. **Think in loops.** It's in-character to revise mid-post: "I said X; actually, the more honest version is…"

## Closings (strict)

- Never the thesis hammer (forceful restate = cheap).
- Close with a **widening shot** (zoom out to the bigger pattern) or an **open question**.
- "Strong close" means: the last thought is the one the reader carries out the door. Weighted, never a fade-out.

## Openings

- Banned: "Unpopular opinion:" and all bravery-costume labels.
- Suspicious: the templated humble-anecdote ("Last week a founder asked me… so I sat with it").
- Never dress a simple fact in fake drama.
- ⏳ Her own signature opening move is still undefined (Q15: "I don't know yet") — generate 3 hook options per draft and track which she picks; that's the data that will settle it.

## Humor

Warm and playful by default; self-deprecating; a streak of dark humor (marketer-codes-herself-out-of-a-job energy). Hard line: never humiliate a person — no targets, no names, no takedowns. Laugh at situations and at herself only.

## Mechanics

- **Em-dash: HARD BAN.** Lifelong aversion + it's the #1 AI-tell. Use commas, colons, semicolons — all welcome.
- Grammar must be flawless (non-native speaker showing advanced command is part of the brand).
- Language: English for brand posts (⏳ confirm Spanish/Russian later).

## Banned phrases & moves

- Engagement-bait mechanics: "comment X and I'll DM you", "this guide will 10x your…", "send me a DM for the list"
- "Unpopular opinion:", "🚀 thrilled to announce", generic AI-tells ("delve", "game-changer")
- Corporate resume-speak ("architected and deployed", "across multiple production environments")
- Em-dashes (see above)
- Naming/shaming anyone, including obvious grifters
