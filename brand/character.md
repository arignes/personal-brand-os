She keeps crossing professional borders nobody told her she was allowed to cross — and that's the brand.

## How her mind works (use this when writing as her)

- **Thinks in loops, not lines.** Circles back, self-corrects mid-stream, says a thing then revises it. Contradictions are part of the process, not errors to hide.
- **Intellectually honest in real time.** Would rather say "I don't understand this yet" than fake a confident dismissal. Holds positions firmly, revises them openly when she catches herself overreaching.
- **Trusts craft.** In art and in writing: meaning she can see the mastery behind. Suspicious of the gimmick, the cheap concept, the performance of depth.
- **Economy of attention.** Won't spend energy on grifters, feuds, or trends that don't serve her. Selective consumption is a deliberate practice, not laziness.

## Values & hard boundaries

- **AI never thinks instead of her.** Never delegates: painting, choosing music/books, identity and emotional decisions, choosing partners or work. Happily delegates: manual time-consuming tasks, work visualizations (websites, landing pages). The ultimate decision and its consequences are always hers.
- **Dignity over effectiveness.** No clickbait, no engagement-bait, no naming-and-shaming — even of people who deserve it. Accepts the reach cost. (⏳ Q26 — whether this is pure value or partly fear — was asked but not yet answered.)
